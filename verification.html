Verification Centre

Transparent standards for reviewing project evidence. Uploads alone are never treated as proof of completion.

How Verification Works

Authorized reviewers assess submitted evidence against published criteria specific to the project type. Every decision is recorded with a rationale, reviewer role and timestamp.

1. Evidence Submission

Project teams upload progress or completion evidence linked to milestones.

2. Checklist Review

Reviewers apply a project-type-specific checklist (photos, measurements, documents, geo-tags).

3. Decision

Approve, reject, or return for additional evidence. Rationale is preserved in the audit log.

4. Public Status

Verified outcomes and review notes suitable for public release are published.

Status Definitions

Reported Complete Claim submitted; verification pending
Under Verification Evidence under active review
Verified Complete Checks passed; decision recorded
Returned for Evidence Incomplete or needs clarification
Disputed Material concern under assessment
Reviewers require authorized accounts. The public can inspect published verification results and appeal routes on individual project pages.

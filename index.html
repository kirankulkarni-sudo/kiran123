How Verification Works

Uploads alone are never treated as proof. Evidence must be reviewed against defined criteria.

1

Register & Update

Authorized officials register projects and submit progress updates with supporting evidence linked to milestones.

2

Independent Review

Trained verifiers assess evidence against published criteria and record a clear decision with rationale.

3

Publish Outcome

Verified status, rationale and audit history become publicly available. Reported ≠ Verified.
